title: TfL Status
description: "London line status — Tube, Elizabeth line, DLR and Overground — with the reason behind any disruption a tap away."
---

The London counterpart to [NYC Subway](/docs/widgets/commute/subway/): each
line you pick as a colored dot with **Good Service** or its current
disruption, covering the Tube, the Elizabeth line, the DLR and the
Overground.

**Tap the card** for the full status board; tap a disrupted line there for the
complete reason.

## Configure

**Settings → TfL Status.** Toggle lines on and off, grouped by mode.
