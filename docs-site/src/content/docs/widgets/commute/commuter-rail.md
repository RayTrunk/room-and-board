Two widgets, one shape: a live departure board with each train's line (as a
colored chip in the railroad's own colors), destination, minutes to departure,
and track number when assigned. A service-alert banner appears when the
railroad has something to say — and the banner is its own tap target, opening
the full alert text.

- **LIRR** departs from **Penn Station** (the default), **Grand Central**, or
  **both** — with each train tagged by terminal when you choose both.
- **Metro-North** departs from **Grand Central**.

**Tap the card** for the full board — every fetched departure, not just what
fits.

## Configure

Each has its own Settings section. The one required choice: **the station
your trains must stop at**. The card shows only trains that stop there, names
the station in its corner, and prompts until you've picked one. The alert
banner can be toggled off.

:::note[Why one page for two widgets]
They're twins — same board, same options, different railroad. Add either or
both; each is its own card with its own station.
:::
