Live arrivals for up to two route-and-stop picks, shown in minutes or in
distance ("3 stops away").

**Tap the card** for the full arrivals list.

## Configure

**Settings → Express Bus.** Choose an express route (QM, BM, SIM or X), then
the direction, then your stop — twice, if you want two.
