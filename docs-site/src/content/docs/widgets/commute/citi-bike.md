Live availability at up to six Citi Bike stations: how many bikes (e-bikes
called out separately) and how many open docks at each.

**Tap the card** for all your stations at full size.

## Configure

**Settings → Citi Bike.** Search for stations by their cross-streets ("W 52 St
& 11 Ave"). The card starts with a few sensible defaults you can replace.
