title: NYC Subway
description: "Line-by-line service status for the subway lines you ride, with problems floating to the top."
---

A status board for the subway lines you pick: each line's colored bullet with
either **Good Service** or the current alert. Lines with problems float above
the quiet ones, so trouble is always at the top of the card.

**Tap the card** for the full status board with every alert's complete text.

## Configure

**Settings → Subway.** Tap line bullets to choose your lines. Picking a line
automatically covers its shuttle and express variants, so you don't have to
hunt for them.
