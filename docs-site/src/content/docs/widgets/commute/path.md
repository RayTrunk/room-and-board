The next PATH trains at one station, each as its line's colored dots with the
minutes until it leaves. Show one direction or both.

This is one of the few cards with nothing behind a tap — what you see is the
whole story.

## Configure

**Settings → PATH.** Pick your station (named in the card's corner) and
direction.
