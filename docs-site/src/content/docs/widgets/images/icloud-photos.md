A rotating slideshow from an **iCloud Shared Album** — your photos, on your
board, without any account connected to the board itself. On the dashboard
the card is titled simply "Photos".

**Tap the card** for full screen; swipe to browse. It can also drive the
[screensaver](/docs/using/screensavers/).

## Configure

From your phone at **idlescreen.app/photo-setup** — the widget's Settings
section shows a QR straight to it. The page walks you through creating the
shared album, checks your link actually works, and gives you a short code to
type into the board. The code changes only your photo setup and touches
nothing else on your dashboard.

:::caution[The album is link-shared]
An iCloud Shared Album works by public link — anyone who has the link can see
the photos. Choose office-appropriate ones.
:::

Want a second, separate album? [GDrive Photos](/docs/widgets/images/gdrive-photos/)
is an independent card — run either or both.
