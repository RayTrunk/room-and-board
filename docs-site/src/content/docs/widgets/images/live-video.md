---

A live video stream playing (muted) as a card. It accepts either:

- a **UniFi Protect share-livestream link** (`monitor.ui.com/…`), or
- your own **HLS stream** — an `https` link ending in `.m3u8`.

No stream comes built in; this card only appears once you've given it one.

This is one of the few cards that doesn't expand on tap — it plays where it
sits.

## Configure

Paste and preview your link at **idlescreen.app/video-setup** on your phone
or desktop — it checks the stream actually plays, then gives you a short code
to type into the board. Like the photo codes, it changes only this card.

:::tip[Sound]
Cards are silent by default. If you want this stream audible, the board's
signage audio setting must also be on — see
[Point your board at idlescreen](/docs/board/point-your-board/).
:::
