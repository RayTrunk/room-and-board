A rotating slideshow from a **public Google Drive folder**. It's fully
independent of [iCloud Photos](/docs/widgets/images/icloud-photos/) — its own
album, its own rotation — so you can run either or both. On the dashboard the
card is titled simply "Photos".

**Tap the card** for full screen; swipe to browse. It can also drive the
[screensaver](/docs/using/screensavers/).

## Configure

From your phone at **idlescreen.app/photo-setup** — the widget's Settings
section shows a QR straight to it. The walkthrough covers creating the
folder, sharing it by link, checking it works, and gives you a short code for
the board. One detail specific to Drive: photos must sit directly in the
folder, not in subfolders.

:::caution[The folder is link-shared]
A link-shared Drive folder is visible to anyone holding the link. Choose
office-appropriate photos.
:::
