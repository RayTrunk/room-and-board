The newest finance and business stories, merged across the sources you enable
— MarketWatch, WSJ Markets, FT Markets, CNBC, NYT Business and Yahoo Finance
on by default, Seeking Alpha opt-in. When several outlets cover the same
story, the card keeps the freshest telling and drops the duplicates.

**Tap the card** for the reading list; tap a story for its summary and a QR
code that hands the article to your phone.

## Configure

**Settings → Markets News.** Toggle sources on and off.
