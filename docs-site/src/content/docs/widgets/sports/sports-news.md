---

The newest sports stories, merged across the outlets you enable — ESPN, CBS
Sports, Yahoo Sports and The Athletic by default, BBC Sport and Guardian
Sport opt-in. Duplicate coverage of the same story collapses to the freshest
telling.

Two ways to narrow it:

- **Sport chips** filter the card to particular sports (MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL,
  MLS, F1, Golf, Tennis).
- **Only my teams** goes further: it keeps only stories that name a team you
  follow on [My Teams](/docs/widgets/sports/my-teams/). While it's on, the
  sport chips are disabled.

**Tap the card** for the reading list; stories open with a summary and a QR
code for your phone.

## Configure

**Settings → Sports → Sports News.** Sources, sport chips, and the
only-my-teams switch.
