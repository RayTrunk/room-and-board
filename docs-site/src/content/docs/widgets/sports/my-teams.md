One row per team you follow: the live score while they're playing, the final
when they're done, or the next game with the last result — whichever is true
right now.

**Tap the card** for the full board across all your teams.

## Configure

**Settings → Sports → My Teams.** Follow up to six teams across MLB, NFL,
NBA, NHL, MLS and the Premier League.
