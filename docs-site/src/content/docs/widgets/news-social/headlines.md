The newest stories, merged across the outlets you enable — a selection of NYT
sections (Top Stories, U.S., Business, New York), NPR News, BBC World and
Gothamist. When several outlets cover the same story, the freshest telling
wins and the duplicates drop.

**Tap the card** for the reading list. A story opens with its headline,
summary and a QR code — scan it to keep reading on your phone.

## Configure

**Settings → Headlines.** Toggle outlets on and off.
