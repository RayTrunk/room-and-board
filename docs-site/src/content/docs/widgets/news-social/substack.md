The latest posts from up to six Substack publications you follow. Unlike the
news cards, nothing is deduplicated or filtered here — you chose these
authors, so you see everything they publish, newest first.

**Tap the card** for the reading list; posts open with a summary and a QR
code for your phone.

## Configure

**Settings → Substack.** Type the publication's name — the part before
`.substack.com`.
