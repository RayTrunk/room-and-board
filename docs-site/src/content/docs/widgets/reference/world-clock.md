---
title: World Clock
description: "Up to ten cities ordered by their current time, with tomorrow marked."
---

Up to ten cities, always ordered by their current time, with a marker on any
city already in tomorrow. The defaults — New York, San Francisco, London,
Hyderabad, Hong Kong — are replaceable like anything else.

**Tap the card** to see every city you follow at full size.

Your World Clock cities also power the **World clocks**
[screensaver face](/docs/using/screensavers-and-display-modes/).

## Configure

**Settings → World Clock.** Add offices or any time zone, up to ten.
