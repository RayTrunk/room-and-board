**Settings → Screensaver** picks what fills the screen when the board is idle.

## The options

**Slideshows**

- The Art rotation (public domain works from the Met, the Art Institute of
  Chicago and Cleveland)
- The curated **Landscapes** folder
- Either photo widget's album (iCloud or Google Drive)

**Clock faces**

- **Big clock**, a giant digital time and date
- **World clocks**, an analog dial per World Clock city, night cities dimmed
- **Clock + world times**, a digital hero with a city row

**Off**

## Previews

Every option has a full-screen **Preview**. Tap anywhere to exit.

## The three toggles

| Toggle | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Info strip | The bottom digest: weather plus next departures |
| Hour markers | The clock faces' tick marks |
| Backdrop image | Puts a curated photo behind a clock face, one picture per day, picked deterministically |

## When it appears

The screensaver shows when Display mode is **Always screensaver**, or when it is
**Scheduled** and you are outside the dashboard windows. See
[display modes](/docs/guides/make-it-yours/#display-modes).

## It falls back rather than blanking

If a photo source loses its album, the board falls back to Art, and then to the
Big clock. The screen never goes blank.

Clocks repaint once per minute, aligned to the minute boundary rather than to
whenever the page happened to load.
