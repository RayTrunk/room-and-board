---
title: Set up your board
description: "Three ways to build your dashboard: right on the glass, from your phone, or from your desktop browser."
---

A board pointed at idlescreen greets you with a welcome screen. From there you
have three roads, and they all end in the same place. None of them needs an
account.

## The fastest road: Quick start, on the glass

Tap **Quick start** on the welcome screen. The board starts with a sensible
default dashboard immediately, and you fine-tune it by touch: the ✎ pencil
rearranges the cards, the ⚙ gear opens settings.

Good for: getting something on the screen right now.

## The comfortable road: your phone

1. Scan the **QR code** on the welcome screen with your phone. It opens the
   setup page.
2. Walk through the picker: choose your widgets, your weather location, your
   stations and lines.
3. Tap **Get my setup code**. You get six characters.
4. On the board, tap **I have a setup code** and type them in.

Good for: building the whole thing comfortably from your chair, then applying
it in one go.

## The roomy road: your desktop

The same setup page works in any browser — go to **idlescreen.app/setup** on
your computer. Build the full configuration with a keyboard and a big screen,
tap **Get my setup code**, then walk over and type the six characters into the
board (welcome screen → **I have a setup code**, or on a configured board:
⚙ gear → **Setup code**).

Good for: the full custom build, or setting up several boards to a pattern.

:::note[About the code]
A setup code lives for one hour and works once. Nothing breaks if it expires —
just generate a new one. More in [Setup codes explained](/docs/codes/setup-codes/).
:::

## After setup

Everything stays editable on the touchscreen — you never need the setup page
again unless you want to rebuild from scratch or
[back your board up](/docs/codes/back-up-your-board/). Later tweaks are
usually easier directly on the glass.
